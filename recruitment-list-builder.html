How roles work

PNM — the person being recruited in this round.
Blue (Rd 1 & 3) — recruiter in rounds 1 and 3 (Philanthropy & Sisterhood).
White (Rd 2) — recruiter in round 2 (Philanthropy & Sisterhood).
Soft / Hard — the two recruiter roles in Preference round.
QB — the quarterback who guides the conversation group.

A person who appears in both blue columns and the white column is called a Violet and is highlighted purple. In Preference, someone in both Soft and Hard is also highlighted purple.
